Victor Cajiao is now retired from podcasting, but he produced and created many shows over the years. He started with the Typical PC User Podcast before switching to the Mac and starting the Typical Mac User Podcast. Later he added photography podcasting to his repetoir with the Typical Shutterbug Podcast, before finishing with a more generic technology show, the Terra Tech podcast. As well as producing his own shows, Victor was also one of the regular contributors to the Mac Roundtable Podcast.
