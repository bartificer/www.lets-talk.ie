Tim was the founder of the Stoplight Podcasting Network, and the father of the My Mac podcast. The Stoplight Network, later the My Mac Podcasting Network was the original home of both Let's Talk Apple and Let's Talk Photography, and without Tim, neither show would have ever gotten off the ground.

Sadly, Time passed away in 2025.
