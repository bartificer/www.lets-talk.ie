<p>Joining Bart this month are Gazmaz from the MyMac Podcast, Stefaan Lesage from the Tech45 Podcast, and Antonio Rosario & Tom Martinez from Switch to Manual.</p>

<p>With the winter gift-giving season fast approaching, this month's discussion is around how to go about deciding what photography gear to buy. The panel specifically focus on cameras, lenses, bags, harnesses, tripods and monopods.</p>

<p>Next month's show will pick up where we left off this month, moving on to all the other bits and bobs we use when taking photographs.</p>
