As I've mentioned on the show a few times, I've been putting a lot of work into a presentation to the wonderful [Napels Mac Friends MUG](https://naplesmug.com) on getting great photos with your iPhone. I finally gave that talk at the start of the month, and I think it went well. By its very nature much of the talk was very iPhone specific. Since this is explicitly not a gear show, those parts of the slides would not be a great fit here. But, one section of the talk really isn't iPhone specific — the generic advice for getting better photographs, and that's what I'll be sharing here.
