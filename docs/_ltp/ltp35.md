<p>This month Bart is joined by photographer <a href="http://jeffcurto.com/" target="_blank">Jeff Curto</a> talk about the history of photography, and why it matters. Jeff taught a course on photo history in the College of DuPage in Glen Ellyn Illinois for 30 years, and has made his <a href="http://photohistory.jeffcurto.com/" target="_blank">class sessions available as a podcast</a>. Jeff also produces the <a href="http://www.cameraposition.com/" target="_blank">Camera Position podcast</a>, a show about the creative side of photography.</p>
