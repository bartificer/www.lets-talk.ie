This month Bart chats with photographer and podcaster <a href="http://www.cwdaly.com" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Clay Daly</a> from <a href="http://www.cwdaly.com/throughmylens/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">the Through My Lens podcast</a>. The conversation is wide-ranging, but loosely focused around the question of what is and is not photography. The same phrase came up again and again — <em>"the Image has to Speak for Itself"</em>.
