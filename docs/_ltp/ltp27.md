<p>In last month's show, Antonio Rosario recommended small drones as good presents for photographers, because you could use them as practice before moving on to a big drone. Stefaan Lesage reached out to Bart after listening to that episode with the suggestion of dedicating a future show to the topic of drones (or as Stefaan prefers to call them, UAVs). There are bound to be many drones getting unwrapped over the next few weeks, so now seemed like the perfect time, and Bart and Stefaan got together on Skype and recorded this conversation.</p>
