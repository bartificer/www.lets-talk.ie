<p>This month Bart chats with Australian photographer Doug Ingram, better known as Nightscapades, about the art and craft of shooting so-called 'nightscapes'. Depending on the shot and your point of view a nightscape can be described as a landscape that includes the night sky, or a nigh-sky shot that includes at least some of the landscape below.</p>
