<h4>Listener Question</h4>

<p>Question from listener Claus:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Whenever I take a picture of my six year old and she is aware of the camera she'll go stiff and have a "fake smile". She so desperately wants a pretty photo of herself, that it completely takes over.</p>

<p>The best shots are those that I can sneak of her, but those aren't always blessed with the best composition.</p>

<p>So calling all parents, uncles, aunts (etc.) - how can I help my six year old to forget there is a camera and just smile at the bloke behind the camera (a.k.a. dad) as she normally would...﻿</p>
</blockquote>
