<h5>Tips form Mark</h5>

<ul>
<li>Search for &#8220;call for artists&#8221; in your area</li>
<li>Join a local artists guild or group - a great source of advice and wisdom</li>
<li>Keep an eye out for festivals and things like that in your area</li>
<li>Your portfolio should be &#8216;the cream&#8217; of your work, small, and indicative of what you do</li>
<li>Get a portfolio review from a trusted expert who can be honest with you</li>
</ul>
