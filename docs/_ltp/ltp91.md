<h5>Favourite Quotation</h5>



<blockquote>
<p><em>"I can't move the sun — and it's always in the wrong place — and I can't even move the tracks, so I had to create my own environment through lighting"</em></p>
<p style="text-align: right"><strong>— O. Winston Link</strong></p>
</blockquote>
