<p>This month Bart is joined by returning guest and photographer Jeff Curto. This is another instalment in the series-within-a-series where Bart invites photographers he respects to evangelise photographers they respect. The sky is the limit, guests can choose any photographer who's been active at any time between the invention of photography and the present day. Jeff chose to share his love for Olivia Parker's work, a photographer who has been active since the 1970s, and is still active today.</p>
