<p>With the giving season freshly behind us, January seemed like the perfect month to start as two-part series on understanding our cameras. All our cameras, regardless of manufacturer, share the same basic groups of features - they all have different modes for metering light, for focusing, and so on. In this first part we focus mostly on exposure and focusing modes.</p>
