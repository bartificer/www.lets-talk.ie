# Welcome

Hi, my name is Bart, and I host and produce two monthly podcasts on a pair of topics I'm passionate about — **Photography**, and **Apple News**.

Around the middle of each month I publish a discussion related to the art and craft of photography. The aim is to focus on ideas and techniques, and to be compeltely equipment and software agnostic. Regardless of what camera you use, and what your skill level is, you should get something out of the show.

Around the end of the month I publish a 40,000ft view of the month's Apple news. No rumours, no preductions, but a curated big-picture view of Apple's month.
