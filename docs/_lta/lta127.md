<li><p><strong>The DOJ&#39;s 5 Headline Allegations:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Apple does not allow <em>Super Apps</em> in the App Store</li>
<li>Apple unfairly limits game-streaming services</li>
<li>Apple unfairly denies 3rd-party messaging apps access to SMS</li>
<li>Apple unfairly diminishes the functionality of non-Apple smart watches</li>
<li>Apple unfairly limits digital wallets</li>

</ol>
</li>
<li><p>The case has been brought under the Sherman Antitrust Act of 1890(!!!), which doesn&#39;t actually define what a monopoly is, but case law in different courts falls in a range mostly between 70% &amp; 80% with one case saying it might be possible for there to be a monopoly from as low as 55% in some cases.</p>
<ul>
<li>Apple&#39;s current share of units in the US is about 64%, and it&#39;s world-wide unit share is about 23%</li>
<li>The DOJ&#39;s case states Apple&#39;s market provenance not in unit sales as has been the case in all previous suits, but in revenue share, which puts Apple at 65%</li>
<li>The DOJ go further than flipping to a novel measure, and invent a new market <em>&#39;performance smartphones&#39;</em>, and they say Apple has a 70% share of this market</li>

</ul>
