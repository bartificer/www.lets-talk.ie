<li>The NYT Ignited a controversy over Apple&#8217; removal of parental control apps that abused MDM, implying this was some kind of anti-competitive move. Factually, using MDM in a context where the organisation the devices get enrolled into is not the organisation that actually owns the device is extremely dangerous. Here, kids phones were being enrolled into MDM servers managed by the app vendors, giving those vendors total control over the devices. Apple realised the danger back in 2017, and updated its app store guidelines in mid&#8211;2017. Last year Apple started working to address the problem, with reports of apps being removed dating back to last December. It&#8217;s not clear why this has suddenly become news.
