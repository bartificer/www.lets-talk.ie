<h5 id="toc_2">Legal Latest</h5>

<ul>
<li>The US Federal Appeals Court has overturned its own ruling, Samsung does owe Apple $120M for violating Apple’s <em>slide to unlock</em> patent after all - <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/news/samsung-pay-120m/">www.macobserver.com/...</a></li>
<li>The US Supreme Court heard oral arguments in the Apple -v- Samsung case. The question is not whether Samsung violated the patents, but just how expensive that should be for a company. The VW Beetle’s iconic shape played a big part in the discussions - <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/news/supreme-court-apple-samsung/">www.macobserver.com/...</a> &amp; <a href="http://fortune.com/2016/10/11/apple-samsung-scotus/">fortune.com/...</a></li>
</ul>
