## 1 — MapKit for Automotive
- Apple and Ford Partner to bring Apple Maps integration into Ford's own software for their up-coming Fathom electric truck — [📣 Apple PR](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/07/apple-maps-to-power-navigation-experience-for-ford-uev-platform/) & [www.macobserver.com/…](https://www.macobserver.com/news/ford-picked-apple-maps-over-google-maps-for-its-new-ev-and-heres-why/)
- Ford are first, and seem to have helped with the design, but Apple have released this as a whole new API other automotive companies can adopt
- Allows vehicles and Apple Maps to share richer, largely EV-specific, data than regular MapKit:
	- Weather & elevation data to help improve range estimates
	- Charge stop, battery status & preconditioning information to help optimise charging
- Replaces neither traditional CarPlay, nor CarPlay Ultra
- Alan Clarke, Ford's VP of Advanced Vehicle Projects explained that Apple made a more compelling offer than their competitors, including Google
- *"Ford is not bringing CarPlay Ultra to the Fathom. Apple Maps will instead run natively through Ford’s own in-car platform, while standard Apple CarPlay and Android Auto will remain available. That means drivers will still be able to use Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, and other compatible navigation apps through CarPlay while also having Apple Maps built directly into the vehicle.”* — The Mac Observer
- **Related:** [GM pickups' infotainment redesign integrates CarPlay, Android Auto — eu.freep.com/…](https://eu.freep.com/story/money/cars/general-motors/2026/09/15/why-gm-gave-its-popular-pickups-a-new-simplified-infotainment-system/91762793007/)
	- Mirroring services (CarPlay & Android Auto) site side-by-side next to GM's native interface
	- Made possibly by simply adding much bigger screens
	- But only on ICE vehicles: [GM isn't changing course on CarPlay for its electric vehicles — appleinsider.com/…](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/09/21/gm-isnt-changing-course-on-carplay-for-its-electric-vehicles) 🤯
