<p>Joining Bart this month are Guy Serle & Gazmas from the My Mac Podcast, Peter Upfold, and Nick Riley. The show starts with a quick rundown of some interesting numbers released in April, followed by a quick rundown of the month's developments in some of the many court cases Apple are involved in, particularly the second Apple -v- Samsung case, which came to trial in April. The main stories the show focuses on are Apple's Q2 earnings call, Google's Silver program, Amazon's HBO deal & Comixology purchase, the cheaper and faster new Macbook Air, and Apple's focus on all things environmental this month. The show finishes with a few quick stories.</p>
