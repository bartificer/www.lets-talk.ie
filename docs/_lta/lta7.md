<p>Joining Bart this month are Guy Serle from the My Mac Podcast & Allison Sheridan from the NosillaCast Mac Podcast. The show starts with a quick rundown of some interesting numbers released in March, followed by a quick rundown of the month's developments in some of the many court cases Apple are involved in. The main stories the show focuses on are the big changes at Microsoft in March, including the release of Office for iPad, the replacement of the iPad 2 with the iPad 4 at the low end of the iPad range, and some criticism of Apple and other tech companies for the lack of diversity on their boards. The show finishes with a few quick stories.</p>
