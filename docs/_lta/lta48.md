<h5>Main Stories</h5>

<ol>
<li>Tim Cook&#8217;s Apple continues to obviously and actively engaged in public life

<ul>
<li>In a letter to all Apple employees Tim Cook spoke out strongly against hate groups in the aftermath of the terrorist attack and violence Charlottesville, and clearly criticises President Trump&#8217;s response to the attack and the violence - Apple also donates to anti-hate group <em>the Southern Poverty Law Centre</em> (SPLC) and the <em>Anti-Defamation League</em> and starts accepting donation to the SPLC through iTunes - <a href="https://www.imore.com/tim-cook-condemns-hate-pledges-donations-splc-adl">www.imore.com/&#8230;</a> &amp; <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/news/apple-accepting-splc-donations-itunes-now/">www.macobserver.com/&#8230;</a></li>
<li>Apple also moved to removed ApplePay support from websites that sell hate-related paraphernalia including anything with Nazi symbols on it - <a href="https://www.buzzfeed.com/ryanmac/apple-removes-payments-support-from-white-nationalist">www.buzzfeed.com/&#8230;</a> &amp; <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/news/apple-pay-paypal-hate-based-websites/">www.macobserver.com/&#8230;</a></li>
<li>In an interview with the New York Times Tim Cook explains his political philosophy, and explains that he believes business have a moral duty to contribute to society as well as to make money - <a href="http://www.loopinsight.com/2017/08/29/tim-cook-gets-political-with-the-new-york-times/">www.loopinsight.com/&#8230;</a></li>
<li>Tim Cook responds to hurricane Harvey with a letter to all staff explaining how the company is helping victims on Texas, and starts accepting donations for relief to the Red Cross through iTunes - <a href="https://www.buzzfeed.com/ryanmac/heres-what-apple-ceo-tim-cook-had-to-say-about-hurricane">www.buzzfeed.com/&#8230;</a> &amp; <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/news/hurricane-harvey-itunes-store-donations/">www.macobserver.com/&#8230;</a></li>
<li>Apple urged the US Federal Communications Commission (FCC) to establish stronger net neutrality protections - <a href="https://www.imore.com/apple-urges-fcc-establish-stronger-protections-net-neutrality">www.imore.com/&#8230;</a> &amp; <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/news/apple-net-neutrality-comments-fcc/">www.macobserver.com/&#8230;</a></li>
</ul></li>
<li>Apple continues it&#8217;s push into education - specifically into teaching programming

<ul>
<li>Apple makes a deal with more than 30 US college systems to teach Swift to over 74,000 students - <a href="http://www.loopinsight.com/2017/08/25/apple-makes-deal-with-more-than-30-college-systems-to-teach-swift/">www.loopinsight.com/&#8230;</a> &amp; <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/analysis/apples-new-direction-in-education/">www.macobserver.com/&#8230;</a></li>
<li>Apple add support for Sphero&#8217;s new R2-D2 robot to Swift Playgrounds - <a href="https://www.macobserver.com/news/product-news/swift-playgrounds-sphero-r2-d2/">www.macobserver.com/&#8230;</a></li>
</ul></li>
