<p>Joining Bart this month are Allison Sheridan from the NosillaCast, Gazmaz from the MyMac Podcast, and Nick Riley. The show starts with a quick rundown of some interesting Apple-related numbers released in July, followed by a quick rundown of the month's developments in some of the many court cases Apple are involved in. The main stories the show focuses on are Apple's Q3 results, Apple's new partnership with IBM, the (largely inaccurate) reports of a backdoor in iOS, iTunes Pass, Developments at Nadela's Microsoft, and Amazon's new subscription ebook and audiobook service. The show finishes with a few quick stories, including some more Apple hires of note, and improved MacBook Pro models.</p>
