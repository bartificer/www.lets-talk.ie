   - **Parental Controls:** [Apple adds new parental controls across platforms — www.cultofmac.com/…](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/apple-adds-new-parental-controls)
     - Apple's philosophy is to empower parents to parent, rather than having smartphones replace parents
       - *“Our approach to helping families create safer digital experiences is grounded in the belief that every child is unique, that’s why we build simple and intuitive tools, based on expert guidance, to let parents tailor their kids’ digital journey.”* — Dr. Sumbul Desai, Apple VP of Health & Fitness
     - Apple have created a dedicated page for parents: [Child Safety — Apple](https://www.apple.com/child-safety/)
     - Apple developed the suite of tools with the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)
       - Family and child accounts are the corner stone of the system — easier to create child accounts, and they will initialise with expert-recommended defaults
       - Parents decide what apps child accounts can install (including features allowing children to request apps)
       - Parents get to control who kids can contact (through 1st-party apps)
       - Parents can control where kids browse (in Safari at least), including mechanism for kids to request permission for specific sites
       - Screentime have been completely re-designed (so it actually works!) with *allowances*
     - Many of these *'new'* tools are actually proper re-implementation of existing tools that have been frustrating parents for years.
     - Initially, only first-party apps will get good coverage, but Apple have made a whole suite of APIs available to empower developers to extend these features into their apps.

     - 🇪🇺 Understanding the EU Siri AI Exception:
       - The scope of the unavailability is smaller than some headlines imply:
         - It's specifically *Siri AI* that's not available (rather than *Apple Intelligence*)
         - **Only iOS** is affected, because it's designated as a gatekeeper, while macOS is not
         - Because AppleWatch and CarPlay are totally dependent on iOS, they're affected too, but because of technical decisions Apple made, not because they are gatekeepers
       - The EU replied to Apple's press release with their own spin on things: [EU Says Apple Chose Not to Launch Siri AI Instead of Following DMA Rules — www.macobserver.com/…](https://www.macobserver.com/news/eu-says-apple-chose-not-to-launch-siri-ai-instead-of-following-dma-rules/)
         - *"The decision not to roll out Siri AI in the EU is Apple’s and Apple’s only. Apple was simply unable to develop interoperability solutions that meet essential EU privacy and security standards. Instead of trying to find a suitable compliance solution, Apple simply made a request to the European Commission to be exempted from their interoperability obligations under the DMA, and this for at least 18 months. That’s not an option."* — The EU Commission
       - **Analysis:** [The Washington Post on the EU's DMA Folly — daringfireball.net/…](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/06/15/washington-post-dma-folly)

That’s technically true and wholly beside the point. The law requires that the moment Siri AI ships in Europe, any rival AI agent must get the same sweeping access to a user’s messages, files and chat history. Apple proposed putting in a software security layer to make that safe and offered a phased rollout to build it.
